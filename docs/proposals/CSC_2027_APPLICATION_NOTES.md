# CSC 2027 application tailoring notes

## Northeastern University (NEU), Shenyang

Official 2027 Type-B guide: https://studyinneu.neu.edu.cn/html/article_content/202610/71C6675B7C3749DA85379684D8299E44.shtml

Official guide summary (checked 1 November 2026):
- Application period: 1 November 2026 to 31 January 2027.
- NEU agency number: 10145; CSC programme category: Type B.
- Applicants must complete both the CSC system and NEU's international-student application system.
- The study plan/research proposal must be in Chinese or English and contain at least 1,000 words.
- The plan should explain the major or research field intended at NEU.
- The 2027 master's and PhD programme lists are separate annexes. The exact programme title and language of instruction must be checked against the master's list before submitting.
- The guide lists a supervisor acceptance-letter template as an annex. Confirm with NEU whether it is required for the specific master's programme; do not assume a letter from a DMU professor satisfies an NEU requirement.

The draft in NEU_CSC_2027_Study_Plan.md is approximately 1,825 words and exceeds the stated minimum. It is a content draft, not a reproduction of Annex III's exact layout. Before upload, download the official Annex III template from the guide, transfer the content into that template if required, confirm the programme title and language, and update the supervisor/department fit section with verified information.

NEU application portal: https://studyinneu.neu.edu.cn/

## Cross-application consistency

Keep the following consistent in both applications:
- Applicant's name and academic history;
- bachelor's degree, institution, graduation date, and CGPA;
- research title and central research questions;
- honest status of the prototype, benchmark validation, and experiments.

Tailor the supervisor fit, programme title, university motivation, and study plan separately. Do not report planned experiments as completed results. Do not claim that the benchmark has been database-validated until the validation script has been run successfully against the frozen synthetic database.
